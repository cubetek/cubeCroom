## وصف التوليد المستخدم

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a finished primary logo for CubeCroom, a desktop classroom and lesson-preparation application for teachers and students with AI assistance.
Primary request: Design a distinctive, elegant software brand logo combining a cubic form with the idea of an open book / shared learning space. One compact geometric symbol: two folded, interlocking planes with a clear central opening; the silhouette subtly suggests a cube and a C without becoming a literal detailed drawing. Prioritize an instantly recognizable bold silhouette that can also stand alone as an app icon.
Style/medium: refined flat vector-style logo design, precise geometry, restrained slightly softened corners, careful negative space, contemporary professional education/productivity brand.
Composition: a single centered vertical lockup, symbol above the wordmark, harmonious spacing. The wordmark is wider than the symbol. Generous but not excessive transparent margins. Output at high resolution, square canvas. This is the actual isolated logo asset, not a brand presentation board.
Color palette: use the existing application's teal: #0f766e primary and #115e59 darker secondary on the mark; wordmark in #0f172a. Flat solid fills only.
Text (verbatim): "CubeCroom". Exact spelling C-u-b-e-C-r-o-o-m, capital C twice. Premium medium-to-semibold geometric humanist sans serif wordmark, excellent kerning, readable and friendly.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background; all unprinted negative space must be transparent, no opaque white or checkerboard background.
Constraints: one logo only, no tagline, no additional labels, no mockup, no scene, no texture, no shadows, no gradients, no glossy 3D effects, no hairline details, no graduation cap, no robot, no brain, no generic AI sparkle, no watermark. Do not copy existing brands. Deliver a clean usable PNG logo.
```
